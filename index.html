What this does

Twelve sizes are ready to pick, from a 1280 by 720 YouTube thumbnail to 3000 by 3000 podcast art, plus any custom size you type. Replace the logo with your own, and set the headline colours by hand or pull them out of the picture you just dropped in.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no server to send anything to — here is how to check that for yourself.

Where platforms cover your thumbnail

Vertical covers lose far more of the frame than a landscape thumbnail does. TikTok keeps about the right fifth for its buttons and the bottom fifth for the caption, so a headline centred in the frame ends up behind a share button. See the safe area for every size, drawn out.
